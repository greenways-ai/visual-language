Statstrade uses the Visual Language to separate **everyday participation** from **high-concept spatial presence**. Both surfaces read and write the same posts, comments, communities, identities, and live market state.

## The feed remains ordinary on purpose

The default route is a fast, readable community feed. It borrows the useful information architecture of Reddit—ranked streams, communities, nested discussion, voting or conviction, sort modes, visible moderation, and provenance—without copying Reddit's brand or exact components.

Roman architecture does not sit behind every card. Red is used for the angular S, live state, commitment, and resolution. Core participation works on a phone, on a poor connection, and without WebGL.

## The arena has its own day and night

The world is entered from a post, live market, or explicit **Enter world** action. These paired study plates lock the same amphitheatre, floor geometry, standards, camera, and embedded S while exploring two operating states.

These are art-direction plates and loading fallbacks. The production world remains a real Gaussian-splat capture or render, cleaned and exported as `.sog` or streamed `lod-meta.json` content.

## Spatial product grammar

| Product concept | Feed surface | World surface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Community name and rail | Standard, district, or gathering place |
| Live prediction | Compact probability module | Marked position on the arena floor |
| Evidence | Link, image, or receipts | Accessible record at a spatial marker |
| Resolution | Result post | Sealed gate, lowered standard, or processional state |
| Navigation | Link and scroll position | Camera bookmark and hotspot |

**Enter arena** preserves the current post or market as the spatial destination. **Return to feed** restores discussion context and scroll position.

## No robot imagery

AI and automation are infrastructure, not characters. The world uses people, crowds, architecture, standards, documents, scoreboards, mosaic, and real material systems. It forbids robots, androids, mechs, AI avatars, chrome heads, synthetic gladiators, and generic blue hologram control rooms.
