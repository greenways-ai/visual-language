Statstrade demonstrates why the Visual Language is a system rather than a visual skin.

## Feed

The everyday product is a dense, text-first, community-led feed:

- ranked posts and communities;
- nested discussion;
- compact live predictions;
- evidence and resolved outcomes;
- a clear **Enter arena** action when spatial context adds value.

Roman imagery does not sit behind every feed card. The angular red S and disciplined red status language provide identity while the information remains primary.

## World

The second surface is an opt-in spatial environment called **The Arena of Conviction**. It uses intact Roman civic architecture, red standards, basalt, travertine, porphyry, bronze, human crowds, and physically embedded S marks.

The production target is a real Gaussian-splat scene mounted with the official SuperSplat viewer. The committed SVG is a concept poster and fallback, not a fake splat.

## One data model

A post can open a world camera bookmark and hotspot. A world event can return to the feed as a normal post or result. Identity, comments, and market state remain application data rather than being buried inside a 3D engine.

## No robot imagery

AI and automation are infrastructure, not characters. The visual contract explicitly forbids robots, androids, mechs, powered armour, AI avatars, chrome humanoid heads, cyberpunk control rooms, and robot gladiators.
