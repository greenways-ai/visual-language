A sigil is a compact, named piece of project identity. Its geometry should remain recognisable without a wordmark, but its mosaic construction is equally important.

Greenways now uses a dedicated master geometry rather than assigning itself one of the sixty exploratory studies. See [Greenways master mark](./greenways-master-mark/) for the locked five-petal peacock-tail / lotus system.
