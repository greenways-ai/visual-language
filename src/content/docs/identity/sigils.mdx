A sigil is a compact, named piece of project identity. Its geometry should remain recognisable without a wordmark, but its mosaic construction is equally important.

Greenways now uses a dedicated master geometry rather than assigning itself one of the sixty-two exploratory studies. See [Greenways master mark](./greenways-master-mark/) for the locked five-petal peacock-tail / lotus system.

## Dimensional studies

Project sigils may become reliefs, solid objects, or carefully authored assemblies while retaining their canonical square-on silhouette and smalti construction.

The [3D mark system](./3d-marks/) defines the shared geometry, material, motion, and fallback contract. Use the [3D Mark Lab](./3d-mark-lab/) to compare every canonical project motif under the same camera and shader rig before committing to model topology.

A dimensional treatment may reveal project character through side walls, bevels, light, and construction. It must not add literal symbols, morph one sigil into another, or depend on animation for recognition.
