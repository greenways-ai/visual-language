Artwork is organised as **places**, not decorative backgrounds. Each scene records its world, architecture, affordance, day behaviour, night behaviour, camera, crop focus, and optional architectural sigil.

## World identities

- **Greenways** — civic gardens, observatories, water courts, and living architecture.
- **Hestia** — protected domestic space, thresholds, recovery, provision, and correspondence.
- **Hoplite** — fast routes, open gates, distributed pavilions, and fortified gardens.
- **Historia** — evidence chambers, libraries, archives, and source comparison.
- **Hodos** — theatres, mirrors, veils, lanterns, and optical passages.
- **World / WWW** — large connected ecologies without one mascot or dominant monument.
