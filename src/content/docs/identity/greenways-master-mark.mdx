title: Greenways master mark
description: The canonical five-petal peacock-tail and lotus mosaic for Greenways.
---

The Greenways master mark is a restrained **peacock-tail / lotus hybrid** built from five converging fields of irregular Voronoi tesserae.

The generated SVG assets are the production source of truth for the approved silhouette, spectral balance, and material character.

## Locked direction

- **Five petals, one base point.** The mark opens like a small peacock tail while retaining the calm symmetry of a lotus.
- **No embedded project sigils.** Greenways represents the whole environment without turning the master mark into a catalogue of sub-brands.
- **Tessellation is structural.** Every field is made from irregular Voronoi/smalti pieces. The pattern is not a texture applied after the shape is drawn.
- **Multi-spectral, not rainbow.** Emerald, aquamarine, cyan, peacock blue, and violet form one controlled spectrum.
- **Calm before ornate.** Gold, outlines, rings, eyes, and decorative filigree are not part of the primary mark.

## Geometry and colour

The outer emerald pair provides the broad living base. Aquamarine and cyan rise through the middle as connected planes. A violet centre gives the mark its vertical lift and distinguishes it from the project-specific lotus used by Visual Language.

All five planes converge at the same lower point. This shared origin is important: the mark should read as one connected system, not five independent leaves.

## Production assets

Canonical variants are generated into `assets/favicons`:

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `greenways.svg` | Adaptive detailed mark |
| `greenways-light.svg` | Fixed light-context mark |
| `greenways-dark.svg` | Fixed dark-context mark |
| `greenways-small.svg` | Adaptive compact mark |
| `greenways-small-light.svg` | Fixed compact light mark |
| `greenways-small-dark.svg` | Fixed compact dark mark |

The compact files keep the same five-petal geometry and use larger Voronoi tesserae. They must never become a flat silhouette.

## Wordmark relationship

The emblem and the `Greenways` wordmark are separate assets. Use the current typography tokens for the wordmark rather than converting exploratory lettering into paths. The emblem may appear alone in a favicon, project switcher, application tile, or compact navigation control.

## Do not

- place project sigils inside the petals;
- replace the Voronoi cells with gradients or regular square tiles;
- add a peacock eye, compass, gem, ring, or gold outline to the primary mark;
- flatten compact variants;
- recolour individual cells arbitrarily outside the canonical spectral palette.
